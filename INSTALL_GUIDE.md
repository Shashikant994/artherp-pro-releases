# ArthERP Pro — Installation & Setup Guide

This guide walks you through installing and configuring ArthERP Pro on a fresh Windows PC.

---

## Step 1: Download & Run the Installer
1. Download **`ArthERP_Pro_Setup.exe`** from the repository's `releases/` folder or GitHub Releases.
2. Double-click the installer. If Windows SmartScreen prompts:
   - Click **More info**
   - Click **Run anyway**
3. Follow the wizard and click **Install**.

---

## Step 2: First-Time Launch & Configuration
1. Double-click the **ArthERP Pro** icon on your Desktop.
2. On first launch, the 30-Day Full Feature Trial activates automatically.
3. Open **Settings & Profile** from the left navigation bar:
   - Enter your **Business Trade Name** and **Legal Entity Name**.
   - Select your **State Code** and **Taxation Scheme** (Regular, Composition, or Unregistered).
   - Enter your **GSTIN** (if registered) and **10-digit Official Mobile Number**.
   - Add your **Bank Name, Account Number, IFSC**, and **UPI ID** (required for dynamic invoice QR codes).
   - Click **Upload Logo** (max 1.0 MB) and **Upload Signature** (max 500 KB).
   - Click **Save Business Profile**.

---

## Step 3: Start Billing
- Press **F1** or click **+ New Bill** to open the POS Billing Terminal.
- Scan barcodes or type item names for instant search (<20ms).
- Choose your preferred print template (A4, A5 Smart Adaptive, or 80mm/58mm Thermal).
- Click **Print & Save** (`F12`) to issue the bill!
