## ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

### 🦚 1. Signature Peacock Feather Theme (मोरपंखी)
- **Eye-Soothing Palette:** Engineered with deep peacock teal (`#0F766E` / `#0D9488`), soft mint light mode, and midnight ocean dark mode.
- **Zero Eye Strain:** Specially tuned for long 10–12 hour counter shifts without visual fatigue.
- **Ultra-Crisp Typography:** Full hinting and anti-aliasing on Windows 100%, 125%, and 150% HiDPI displays.

### 🧾 2. Smart Adaptive Print Invoices & Receipts
- **A5 Portrait (Smart Adaptive):** 1–12 items fit cleanly onto 1 single page; 13+ items automatically transition to a multi-page layout with zero paper waste.
- **Dual Printing Engines:** Supports Full Color and Pure B&W Laser-Safe mode (0 color bleed on toner).
- **All Formats Covered:** Classic A4, Modern A4, Stock/Tally A4, A5 Portrait, 80mm Thermal, and 58mm Thermal receipts.
- **Instant Dynamic UPI QR:** Centered statutory scan-and-pay UPI QR code printed automatically on all bills.

### 💼 3. Comprehensive Indian GST Compliance
- **All Tax Schemes:** REGULAR (Tax Invoice with CGST/SGST/IGST, ITC-ready), COMPOSITION (Bill of Supply, CMP-08), and UNREGISTERED (Non-GST Cash Memo).
- **Automated Filing Data:** 1-Click generation of GSTR-1, GSTR-3B summary, and GSTR-2B purchase ITC reconciliation.
- **E-Way Bill Generation:** Instant statutory JSON generation for ₹50,000+ consignments.

### ⚡ 4. Fast Offline Database & Crash Recovery
- **SQLite WAL & FTS5:** High-speed full-text search (<20ms) across 50,000+ items and instant POS billing.
- **Draft Cart Auto-Save:** Active cart lines persist on every keystroke — if power fails, cart recovers automatically on restart.
- **Automated AES-256 Backups:** Daily encrypted backups (`.arthbak`) created on app close.

## 📖 Quick Start Guide

1. Download **`ArthERP_Pro_Setup.exe`** from the link above or GitHub Releases.
2. Run the installer and click **Install**.
3. Launch **ArthERP Pro** from your Desktop shortcut.
4. Go to **Settings & Profile** (`F10`) to enter your business name, address, GSTIN, and UPI ID.
5. Upload your **Firm Logo** (recommended 300×120px) and **Authorised Signature** (recommended 300×150px).
6. Press `F1` or click **+ New Bill** to start billing!
